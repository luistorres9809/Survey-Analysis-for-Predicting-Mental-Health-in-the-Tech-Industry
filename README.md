# 1.Introduction

Mental health of employees has emerged as a crucial aspect in organizations where the mental stress, pressure, and a high-productivity culture can lead to emotional instability. The World Health Organization has described mental health as an optimal state that allows an individual to cope with the usual demands of life, grow in their occupation, and become actively engaged in the social environment (World Health Organization, 2022). However, some results show that factors such as chronic work stress, overloading of work, or lack of institutional support have adverse effects on such status 

Despite the efforts to de-stigmatize mental health, one of the strongest barriers to seeking help continues to exist, perpetuated to some degree by the perceptions of judgment, discrimination, or job-related consequences. Machine learning holds a methodological solution to help predict help-seeking in this regard. Such technology holds the ability to generate predictive models to deal with large data sets and detect subtle relationships between individual, organizational, and behavioral factors 
Open Sourcing Mental Illness (OSMI, 2023) is a non-profit organization whose mission is to encourage education and support for mental illness across tech communities. OSMI has been conducting anonymous surveys of technology workers on matters of mental health at their workplaces for several years.

This project proposes using a machine learning model based on real surveys carried out by OSMI to create an analytical framework that helps in understanding and predicting the behaviors of employees about their mental well-being, such that other companies can take action to help their employees. In addition to helping identify meaningful patterns, this approach generates meaningful knowledge for organizations that wish to establish evidence-informed prevention and early intervention strategies.
